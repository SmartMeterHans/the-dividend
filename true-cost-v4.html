    Methodology & Data Sources

    All figures use conservative estimates. Where ranges exist, lower bounds are used. AI-generated conflict analyses are clearly labelled and explicitly invite peer review and correction. This model is open to challenge, improvement, and independent verification.

    World Bank — Country Economic Monitors
    IMF — Article IV Consultation Reports
    SIPRI — Military Expenditure Database
    UNHCR — Global Displacement Reports
    ACLED — Armed Conflict Location & Event Data
    UN OCHA — Humanitarian Response Plans

    THE PEACE DIVIDEND PROJECT
    This tool exists because peace has never had the same analytical infrastructure that war has. Every number here is sourced, conservative, and open to scrutiny. We invite economists, researchers, and journalists to improve this model. The methodology is public. The goal is clarity, not advocacy.
