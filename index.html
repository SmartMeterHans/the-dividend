About the Project
What this is.
And what it isn't.

The Peace Dividend Project is an independent initiative to build the analytical infrastructure that peace has never had — and to make it freely available to every citizen, journalist, policymaker, and researcher on earth.

Every war has economists modelling its costs and benefits. Every military programme has analysts justifying its budget. Peace has never had the same rigour applied in its favour. This is our attempt to change that.

Not a campaign. Not affiliated with any political party or government. Our aim is that every figure is sourced, every method public and every analysis open to correction. Where we fall short, we say so.

01
Transparent by design. Every number on the home page links to its source and is graded A (primary), B (secondary) or C (indicative). Unverified numbers are flagged, and mistakes are published in the corrections log.
02
Open to challenge. We explicitly invite economists and researchers to find errors. The goal is accuracy, not advocacy.
03
Free, forever. No paywalls. No subscriptions. This is a public resource for anyone who needs it.
04
AI-assisted, human-checked. We use Claude (Anthropic) to draft and research. A number counts as verified only after a human has checked it against its source.
Origin & Team

Initiated in March 2026 by a retired Dutch IT professional with 40 years of international experience — no political connections, no institutional backing, and no agenda beyond one conviction: that the argument for peace deserves the same analytical rigour that war has always enjoyed.
