About the Project
What this is.
And what it isn't.

The Peace Dividend Project is an independent initiative to build the analytical infrastructure that peace has never had — and to make it freely available to every citizen, journalist, policymaker, and researcher on earth.

Every war has economists modelling its costs and benefits. Every military programme has analysts justifying its budget. Peace has never had the same rigour applied in its favour. This is our attempt to change that.

Not a campaign. Not affiliated with any political party or government. Every figure is sourced, every methodology is public, and every analysis is explicitly open to correction.

01
Transparent by design. Every number links to its source. Conservative estimates throughout. Uncertainty flagged where it exists.
02
Open to challenge. We explicitly invite economists and researchers to find errors. The goal is accuracy, not advocacy.
03
Free, forever. No paywalls. No subscriptions. This is a public resource for anyone who needs it.
04
AI-powered, human-verified. We use Claude (Anthropic) as our analytical engine. All AI content is labelled and subject to human review.
