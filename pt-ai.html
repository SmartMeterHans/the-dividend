Plain Truth 02 — The AI Choice

The most powerful
technology ever built.
What are we using it for?

AI can help cure diseases, feed people and find clean water. It can also steer weapons. Both are happening now. Which one grows faster is a choice.

In 2020, a team at Google DeepMind presented an AI model called AlphaFold2. It cracked a problem biologists had worked on for 50 years: predicting how a protein folds from its building blocks. With its help, the structure of proteins has been predicted — virtually every protein researchers have identified.

It is free to use. from 190 countries have used it — to understand antibiotic resistance, and to design enzymes that break down plastic. In 2024 its makers received the Nobel Prize in Chemistry.

This is what AI can do
One AI model changed biology for the whole world — and it was given away.

Where the AI money goes.

Governments are investing too — in war. The US Defense Department alone requested for autonomous and remotely operated military systems in its 2026 budget: drones and vehicles in the air, on land and at sea.

No government publishes a comparable figure for AI used for health, food or water. Nobody counts it. That is part of the problem — and making that comparison possible is part of this project.

We are not short of the technology. We are not short of the science. We are short of the decision to point one at the other.

But isn't military AI necessary for national security?

That's a legitimate question, and we won't pretend it isn't. Countries defend themselves, and AI is now part of that.

But no budget anywhere sets the balance between AI for defence and AI for human welfare on purpose — because the second number isn't even collected. A choice nobody measures is a choice nobody makes. We want that balance to be measured, published and debated.

What we ask
Count it. Then choose it.
Publish what governments spend on AI for health, food, water and rebuilding — next to what they spend on AI for war. Once both numbers are on the table, the direction becomes a decision instead of an accident.
