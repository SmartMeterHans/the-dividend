Who is counted
All individuals with verified net worth ≥ $100M. Source: Forbes 2024 rich lists, UBS/Credit Suisse Global Wealth Report 2023–24, Wealth-X UHNW Report 2023.
What "excess" means
Only wealth above the $100M floor. A person worth $5B contributes $4.9B to the pool. Their first $100M is untouched. Floor = number of people × $100M.
Two scenarios
One-time: Full excess pool redirected once to government. Annual 2%: Piketty/Saez/Zucman proposal — 2% of excess wealth per year, recurring.
Budget impact
Reallocation added proportionally to health, education, and social protection. Defence spend shown for contrast. IMF Government Finance Statistics 2023.

Russia: The Oligarch Paradox
Russia presents the starkest illustration of the wealth-welfare gap of any major economy. It has more ultra-high-net-worth individuals than most Western European nations, yet spends less on health per capita than Poland. Its government dedicates 22.7% of the budget to defence — while its $100M+ cohort holds more offshore wealth than the country spends on health in a decade.

★ The Core Paradox
Russia's 1,650 declared ultra-wealthy individuals hold more excess wealth above $100M than Russia spends on health and education combined in three years. Their estimated true offshore holdings — conservatively $1 trillion — exceed Russia's entire government budget by more than double. The country's citizens live shorter lives than in neighbouring Poland, yet their government spends 23 kopeks of every budget ruble on weapons. This is not a resource constraint. It is a political choice.
