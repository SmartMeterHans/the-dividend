Methodology
How we work with data
These tools are built as a first draft — serious enough to be challenged, transparent enough to be improved. Every figure links to its source. We explicitly invite economists and researchers to identify errors.
Conservative estimates throughoutWhen a range of credible figures exists, we use the lower end. We would rather understate the case than risk overstating it.
Primary sources preferredSIPRI, World Bank, IMF, OECD, EU Tax Observatory, ProPublica IRS data, Wealth-X, UBS Global Wealth Report, and peer-reviewed academic papers.
