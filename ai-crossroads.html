It accumulated — through budget cycles, procurement processes, and the institutional gravity of military-industrial infrastructure built over 80 years.

Section 06 — The Mind Shift
You have the skills.
Here is what else
you could build.

Written directly to the people who build AI systems. Most of you went into this field to build things that matter. Here is what your specific skills could do if pointed in a different direction.

The technology is the same.
The training is the same.
The choice is different.

If you work in computer vision: the same algorithms that identify targets can identify tumours, track disease vectors, and detect structural failure in buildings before earthquakes collapse them. The code is almost identical. The outcome is entirely different.

If you work in reinforcement learning: the same methods that train autonomous weapons can optimise vaccine cold chains, route emergency food aid, and allocate medical resources during pandemics — with a precision that saves thousands of lives per decision.

We are not asking you to abandon your career. We are asking you to consider where it points.
